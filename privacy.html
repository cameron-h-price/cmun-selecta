Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This website

cmunselecta.com is a static site. It doesn't use cookies, analytics or forms, and it doesn't collect personal data. Embedded players and links (YouTube, SoundCloud, Instagram and others) follow those services' own privacy policies.

ytstats (YouTube analytics tool)

ytstats is a private tool used only by CmunSelecta to read analytics for the CmunSelecta YouTube channel. It asks Google for read-only access (yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly) and never changes anything on YouTube.

  • The data it reads is the channel's own aggregated statistics (views, traffic sources, audience, retention, impressions).
  • That data and the sign-in token are stored only on CmunSelecta's own computer. They are not shared, sold or sent to any other service.
  • It is not offered to other users, and it doesn't access any other Google account's data.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
